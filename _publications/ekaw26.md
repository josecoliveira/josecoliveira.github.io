## Abstract

Axiom weakening is a technique that allows for a fine-grained repair of inconsistent ontologies. When repairing ontologies, the current approach chooses an axiom to weaken via sampling minimally inconsistent sets, and then chooses a weakening at random to replace it. In this paper, we propose a method to guide the repair process by using the Shapley inconsistency measure, which is a measure of power index in cooperative game theory.
To repair an inconsistent ontology, this method identifies the axiom that contributes the most towards inconsistency and replaces it by a weaker version that contributes the least. This process is repeated until the ontology is consistent.
We evaluate our approach by comparing the inferable information in the repaired ontologies using our new method against the previous methods with random weakening and removal-based repair.
Our evaluation shows that our approach outperforms previous methods in preserving more inferable information from the original ontology.

**Keywords:** Ontology repair, Axiom weakening, Cooperative games, Power indices, Shapley values.
